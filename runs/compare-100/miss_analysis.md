## Wording points the reviewers raised

None of these changes any key in this run, but two are gaps in the reading rules:

1. **Notices worded as an UPDATE.** Rule 3 names “a CORRECTION notice” and rule 4 “a RETRACTION notice”, but one wording of each is “UPDATE […]: X withdrawn (…)” or “UPDATE […]: magnitude for X revised from … to …”. They are corrections and retractions in substance, but the rules do not name these wordings (seen in H01, H17, H21, O03).
2. **Magnitude scales.** No rule says to compare magnitudes as printed across scales, while a chatter line says “networks report on different magnitude scales (md, ml, mb, mw); values are not converted.” Haiku's answer of 1 in H20/H21 is exactly the one ML-scale quake among 15 qualifying ones. The line was present in all three renderings and Haiku answered the third correctly, so it is at most a partial cause.

Already guarded by the generator (raised, but cannot change a key):

- *Thresholds restated in the other unit are rounded* (e.g. 11.4 mi as “18.3 km”). The margin guards keep every event at least max(0.6 km, 3%) from a depth threshold and max(3 km, 6%) from a radius, far more than the ≤ 0.08 km rounding.
- *“between” / “up to” do not say whether the end points count.* No event lies within 2 minutes of a window edge.
- *UTC offsets are nominal* (whole hours from longitude, e.g. Tonga at UTC−12, not its real +13); every line states its offset, and the rules say to use only the digest.
